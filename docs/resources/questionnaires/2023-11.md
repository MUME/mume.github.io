
# MUME Players - Results 2023

One or two MUs (Manwë Units) after the last one, we are sharing a new questionnaire titled MUME Players - Results 2023.

This has been a community-led effort (see credits) but with Aratar review.

The questionnaire ran from November 2023 through January 2024 for new and old MUME players. 
It is a follow-up questionnaire from the original by [Manwe in 2000](https://www.mume.org).

## Feedback & Insights

### What did you enjoy most about building?
> In conclusion, the building experience in MUME is cherished for its creative freedom, storytelling opportunities, conceptual design, community contribution, learning experiences, and the ability to work on specific projects. This diversity of enjoyable aspects points to a rich and multifaceted building experience within the game.

### What did you enjoy least about building?
> The responses suggest that while there are many enjoyable aspects of building in MUME, there are also significant areas for improvement, particularly in making the process more efficient, user-friendly, and creatively freeing. Reducing wait times for feedback, improving documentation and tools, and addressing the repetitive nature of certain tasks could enhance the overall building experience.

## Client Switching Considerations

> **Are you considering switching Client?**
> The unique responses reveal various reasons why players might consider changing their gaming clients in MUME:
> 
> * **Interest in Mudlet:** Many respondents mentioned considering switching to Mudlet, citing its better scripting/aliasing opportunities and customization options.
> * **Learning Curve and Familiarity:** Some players are hesitant due to the learning curve associated with new clients or difficulty in adapting to them.
> * **Performance:** A few players are considering switching to clients like Powwow or TinTin++ for better performance.
> * **Peer Pressure:** Social influence also plays a role, as some players consider switching due to recommendations or pressure from peers.
> * **Exploring New Features:** The desire for new features and functionalities is another reason for considering a switch.
> * **Technical Advancements:** Some players are open to trying new clients that offer faster performance or more advanced features.

## Why Use Provided Maps vs. Creating Own?
*(Main responses summarized from player feedback)*

* **Time Investment:** Creating custom maps is extremely time-consuming (e.g., maintaining 20k+ room networks).
* **Risk & Danger:** Mapping unexplored or hostile territory in-game carries high risk to characters.
* **Convenience & Quality:** Pre-made maps are accurate, readily available, and allow players to focus on gameplay and exploration rather than cartography.
