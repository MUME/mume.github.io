### Overview & Credits

One or two MUs (Manwë Units) after the last one, we are sharing a new questionnaire titled MUME Players - Results 2023. This has been a community-led effort (see credits) but with Aratar review.

The questionnaire ran from November 2023 through January 2024 for new and old MUME players. It is a follow-up questionnaire from the original by [Manwe in 2000](./2000-04).
