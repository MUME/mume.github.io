### 5. In-Game Statistics & Character Metrics

* **Character Count per Player:** Mean 5.40 (Std Dev: 3.17)
  * *MUME Playerbase Estimate:* ~1,077 global players (5,814 database characters).
* **Ainur (God) Ownership:** 39.61%
* **Darkie Characters:**
  * Average: 2.27 darkies per player (vs. ~3.13 whities/ainur).
  * 22.08% of players owned zero darkies.
* **Legend Characters (Level 26+):**
  * Overall Legends per player: Mean 2.30
  * Darkie Legends per player: Mean 0.91 (57.47% owned zero darkie legends).
* **Highest Mortal Level Reached:** Mean 31.66 (up from 26.5 in 1995).
* **Weekly Hours Played:** Mean 17.94 hours/week (Std Dev: 14.03)
  * Active player estimate (playing > 3 hrs/week): 1,003 players.
