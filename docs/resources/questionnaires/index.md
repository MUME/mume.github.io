# Player Questionnaires & Polls

Over MUME's history, the administration conducted comprehensive player questionnaires to understand player demographics, gameplay habits, client software preferences, and feedback on MUME's game systems and administration.

Choose a questionnaire to view the full survey results and analysis:

- [**April 2000 Questionnaire**](./2000-04)
  *315 respondents* &bull; Demographic shifts, player killing & roleplay frequency, connection types, client software evolution, and OS trends.

- [**February 1998 Questionnaire**](./1998-02)
  *308 respondents* &bull; Demographics, character counts, darkie vs. whitie balance, playtime, client software (rise of zMud), and lag frequency.

- [**October 1995 Questionnaire**](./1995-09)
  *194 respondents* &bull; The inaugural MUME survey covering early player demographics, playing locations, clients (Tintin, Cancan), and MUME population estimates.
