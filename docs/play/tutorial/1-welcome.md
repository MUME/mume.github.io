# Welcome to MUME

**MUME** - *Multi-Users in Middle-earth* - is a **MUD**: a Multi-User Dungeon. It is a game world made entirely of text, played by hundreds of people at the same time, and it has been running and growing since 1991.

The map is Tolkien's Middle-earth, from the Shire to Bree to the Misty Mountains and beyond. You pick a side - the Free Peoples of the West, or the forces of Mordor - create a character, and live in the world: explore it, talk to the people in it, trade, fight, and take on quests.

No installation is required - you can play via our web client. The game shows you a room and its exits in plain words, and you type what you want to do - commands as simple as `look`, `north`, or `say hello`.

This tutorial emulates what your first experience will be like. You will steer a ready-made practice hero named **Fuor** so you can try each command - when you play for real, you will create your own character and choose your own name.
